# AI-Generated Opening Books

These `.tatbook` files contain AI-generated route data prepared for Tetris AT.
The maintainer confirms that the route data was independently generated or
written for this project; public references were used to study strategies,
not to copy third-party route files, artwork or prose. This statement records
provenance; AI generation alone is not a guarantee of originality or ownership.

Tetris AT does not treat a book entry as a verified native template route.
Every suggested placement is translated by occupied cells and must match a
lock returned by Tetris AT's own SRS move generator before it is displayed or
executed. When a state is outside a book's coverage, practice continues with
the General AI.
